Yolk Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Yolk is a nutrition and training tracker published by Ssine Labs. It works entirely on your device. There are no accounts, no servers of ours, no ads and no analytics.

Data we collect

None. Everything you enter, such as meals, weight, goals, workouts and settings, is stored only on your phone. We never receive it and cannot see it.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan barcodes on food packaging. Images are processed on your device and are never saved or sent anywhere.

Network requests to third-party food databases

To look up foods, Yolk sends requests directly from your phone to:

  • USDA FoodData Central: the text you type in food search.
  • Open Food Facts: barcodes you scan and text you type in food search.

These requests contain only the search text or barcode, never your name, identifiers or logged data. As with any internet request, these services can see your IP address and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Yolk schedules local notifications on your device. No push service is involved.

Backups

The backup feature creates a file that you choose to save or share through your phone's share sheet. Nothing is uploaded unless you send the file somewhere yourself.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Yolk or clearing its storage in Android settings deletes all of your data. We hold no copy.

Children

Yolk is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Not medical advice

Yolk is a tracking tool. Calorie and nutrient figures are estimates and are not medical or dietary advice. Consult a professional before making health decisions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
